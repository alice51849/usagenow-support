What is UsageNow?

UsageNow is an AI usage tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It shows how much of each AI plan you have left and exactly when it resets: Claude 5-hour session and weekly limits (including per-model weekly caps), ChatGPT Codex 5-hour and weekly windows, GitHub Copilot premium requests, chat and code completions, and Gemini daily request quotas per model. Every reset has a live countdown.

Who is it for?

Developers who code with AI agents every day and keep running into limits halfway through a task, and anyone juggling several AI plans or accounts, such as work and personal, or Pro and Max, side by side.

How does pricing work?

According to the App Store description, you can see one account's current usage free in the app. UsageNow Pro unlocks more accounts, usage alerts, widgets and Apple Watch with a single one-time purchase. It is not a subscription.

How is it different?

  • Claude, Codex, Copilot and Gemini in one app, with every reset counted down in your local time format.
  • Alerts at the percentage you choose, sent once per limit and cycle, plus an optional alert when a limit is back.
  • Widgets you can flip through account by account and tap to refresh, on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch.
  • No UsageNow server, no account to create and no tracking: you sign in on each service's own page and your sign-in stays in the device Keychain.
  • Fully translated into 50 languages.

Want to try it on your iPhone? UsageNow shows what is left of each AI plan and when it resets, and warns you before you run out.

More questions

Is UsageNow made by Anthropic or OpenAI?

No. UsageNow is an independent app and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub, Microsoft or Google.

Does UsageNow see my conversations?

UsageNow talks only to the services themselves to read usage. There is no UsageNow server and no tracking.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. There is also a Google Play version for Android.
