Where the others are stronger

  • Usage for Claude + ChatGPT says it is used by 30,000+ people and has the deepest history: session charts up to 90 days, a year-long activity grid and monthly insights, synced through your own iCloud. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, alerts you when Claude or ChatGPT is having an incident, and its free version already includes widgets and alerts for one account.
  • Limits: AI Usage Tracker has the most ratings of the four (53) and covers tools UsageNow doesn’t: Cursor, Grok and Antigravity. Its listing says no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your tokens stay in the Keychain on your device. Its latest update adds a 7-day usage history per account, and you can ask Siri how much of your limits you have left and when they reset.
  • Tempo For Claude lists no in-app purchases, puts your Claude Code usage in the Mac menu bar, and adds burn rate, extra usage cost tracking and 24H/7D/30D activity charts.
  • UsageNow has no ratings in the US yet, so you have less to go on from other users.

Where UsageNow is different

  • Four services in one app. Claude (5-hour session and weekly limits, including per-model weekly caps), ChatGPT Codex (5-hour and weekly windows), GitHub Copilot (premium requests, chat and code completions) and Gemini (daily request quotas for each model).
  • Alerts at the number you pick. Choose the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit or all limits, then type the percentage that matters to you, like 90 or 98. Each alert comes once per limit and cycle; a reset alert tells you the moment a limit is back.
  • Widgets that do more. Flip through all your accounts on the widget and tap to refresh. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size, plus Apple Watch complications.
  • No Mac needed. UsageNow runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch on its own, and needs iOS 17 or later.
  • Private by design. You sign in on each service’s own login page, your sign-in stays in your device’s Keychain, and UsageNow talks only to the services themselves. No UsageNow server, no account to create and no tracking.
  • In your language. 44 languages listed on the App Store, with dates and times written the way your region writes them.

What you get without paying in UsageNow

You can see one account’s current usage free in the app. UsageNow Pro unlocks more accounts, usage alerts, widgets and Apple Watch with a single one-time purchase. No subscription.
