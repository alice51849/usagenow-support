Claude weekly limit: how much is left and when it resets

The 5-hour limit is the one you notice, but the weekly limit is the one that can stall you for the rest of the week. If you code with Claude every day, it helps to know how much of the week is left and exactly when it comes back, without opening a settings page in the middle of a task. This page explains how to keep your Claude weekly limit on your iPhone Home Screen and get a heads-up before you run out.

What you see for Claude

For Claude, UsageNow shows the 5-hour session limit and the weekly limits, including per-model weekly caps. Each one comes with a live countdown to its reset and the exact reset time written the way your region writes dates and times.

When does it reset?

Anthropic alone decides what Claude's limits are and when they reset, and the details can change. Rather than guess, UsageNow reads the values for your own account and shows a countdown to each reset, so you can plan bigger tasks for when the weekly limit is back.

Alerts before the weekly limit

Make your own alerts: choose the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit or all limits, then type the percentage that matters to you, like 90 or 98. Add as many as you like. Each alert comes once per limit and cycle, so your Lock Screen stays quiet, and a reset alert tells you the moment a limit is back.

A simple routine: one weekly alert early enough to change how you work for the rest of the week, and one late alert as a final warning.

Several Claude accounts, one screen

Work and personal, Pro and Max, side by side. Sort by 5-hour reset, weekly reset, highest or lowest usage, name or service, or drag accounts into your own order. UsageNow also shows ChatGPT Codex, GitHub Copilot and Gemini if you use them too.

With UsageNow Pro you get Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size, plus Apple Watch complications. Tap a widget to refresh it, and flip through all of your accounts right on the widget. UsageNow runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

You sign in on each service's own login page, your sign-in stays in your device's Keychain, and UsageNow talks only to the services themselves. There is no UsageNow server, no account to create and no tracking.

Do you need an app for this at all?

Not always. You can check your usage in Claude's own settings, with nothing extra to install.
